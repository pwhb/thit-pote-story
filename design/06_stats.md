# Stats

## Goal

The goal is to make a system of different groups of stats that doesn't overlap and is exhaustive.

## Growth (Confirmed)

1. Science
2. Art
3. Love
4. Politics

Explanation: Based on Badiou's four truth procedures, they are the only profound set of virtue that each with free-will should seek.

## Mood (Unconfirmed, might change to a better system)

1. Happy (Valence 75, Arousal 25)
2. Content (Valence 75, Arousal -25)
3. Relaxed (Valence 50, Arousal -50)
4. Calm (Valence 25, Arousal -75)
5. Bored (Valence -25, Arousal -75)
6. Depressed (Valence -50, Arousal -50)
7. Sad (Valence -75, Arousal -25)
8. Distressed (Valence -75, Arousal 25)
9. Angry (Valence -50, Arousal 50)
10. Tense (Valence -25, Arousal 75)
11. Alert (Valence 25, Arousal 75)
12. Excited (Valence 50, Arousal 50)
13. Baseline (Valence N, Arousal M)

Good Emotions
Hopeful (V 60, A 40)
Happy (V 80, A 20)
Serene (V 80, A -20)
Relaxed (V 60 A -40)

Neutral Emotions
Bored (V 40, A -60)
Energized (V 40, A 60)
Tired (V 20, A -80)
Vigilant (V 20, A 80)
Numb (V -20, A -80)
Restless (V -20, A 80)
Weary (V -40, A -60)
Anxious (V -40, A 60)

Bad Emotions
Depressed (V -60, A -40)
Angry (V -60, A 40)
Sad (V -80, A -20)
Distressed (V -80, A 20)

Explanation: Defined by two axes: Valence and Arousal. The baseline of each player is defined by a personality test baked in the introduction session. Mood of the player eventually returns to the baseline even if they do nothing. Various events require various mood and player can use many consumable to adjust their mood.

## Karma (Unconfirmed, but almost stable)

1. Health: rate of Energy recovery
2. Courage: taking risky choices
3. Charm: making socially agreeable choices
4. Diligence: consistency in the moral balance of choices

Explanation: Ledgers to track the player's past actions and judge future actions. It can still be accused to be a hidden rate modifier but the player's choices should have a permanent consequences and this is where it's tracked.
