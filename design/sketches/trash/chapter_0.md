I can't remember the dream well.

Can you call it a betrayal if there was no faith since the beginning?

"May we meet again," we whispered.

It seemed like a movie. Friends and strangers joining hands against a common foe.

Except that there wasn't just one foe. It was us against them and I failed to understand who was us and who was them.

All the four horsemen of the apocalypse were there. I swear I saw them on their mighty mounts.

Did the world end? It did for someone everyday, didn't it?

I'm in here. I'm alive. I'm not sure if it's a good thing.
